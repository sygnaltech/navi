Booking

  • A minimum of 2 adults must be attending the tour for the tour to take place.
  • Application for a tour must be made by phone, fax or e-mail no later than 5:00 PM of the day before the tour.
  • Your application may not be accepted if the tour is full, prior to your booking.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Cancellation of this tour is free if carried out by 5:00 PM, 4 working days before the tour.
  • Cancellation of this tour carried out by 5:00PM from 3-1 working day before the tour will cost 50% of the price of the tour.
  • Cancellation of tours after 5:00 PM 1 working day before the tour will cost the full price of the tour.
* The cancellation after your payment in case of Paypal , does   not apply for a refund of card fees ( 3% ) as per Paypal term   & conditions

☆If you won't show up at the pick-up time even after 15 mins of the time, we have to start the tours without you.

Tour Costs

  • If there are two adults attending the tour, the cost of the tour written is the cost for one person.
  • If there are two adults attending the tour, children ( aged 5~11 ) will cost half of what is written.

Warning

  • Please understand that changes of schedule, cost and tours may occur with no notice.
