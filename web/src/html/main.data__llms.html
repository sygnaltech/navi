Navi Tours

Navi is an Auckland, New Zealand-based tour company which focuses mainly on outdoor throughout New Zealand. We offer Hobbiton tours, adventure tours, family tours, romantic tours, and nature tours, as well as full custom, bespoke tours, and multi-day tours. Our tours are personal, small-group tours to ensure the highest quality experience. We operate as tour guides with the permission of the New Zealand Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

Custom, Bespoke Tours

Customers can design their own unique tour experience, with our help & recommendations. Choose your own dates of travel including half-day, whole day, or multi-day tours. Choose the locations you want to visit, anywhere in New Zealand. Choose the number of people you want to accommodate.

Our custom tours are best suited for groups from 2 to 30+. We can accommodate all ages and situations. We can also support special needs regarding transportation or diet.
