✈️ CAN I TRAVEL ALONE OR DO YOU ONLY ACCEPT GROUP BOOKINGS?

We welcome both individual travelers and groups! You can join the tour from one person, and we can organise large group bookings as well.

English tours are plenty of local excursions to nearby islands such as trekking and popular Waitomo and Rotorua, Bay of Islands resort, and west of Auckland.

‍

🐍 DOES NEW ZEALAND HAVE DANGEROUS ANIMALS?

No. One of New Zealand's most amazing features is that it is completely free of dangerous wildlife. There are no snakes of any kind. No dangerous spiders. No bears, wolves, mountain lions, tigers. You can explore the wilderness in complete safety.

‍‍

🚐 WHERE'S THE MEETING POINT FOR THE TOUR?

Our tours depart from five convenient locations in central Auckland: SkyCity Auckland, JW Marriott Auckland, Cordis Hotel Auckland, Pullman Auckland Hotel, and M Social Auckland.

Please meet your guide in front of the lobby entrance at your selected meeting point. If you experience any difficulty finding the location or meeting your guide, don’t hesitate to contact us at +64 21 462 388 — we’re happy to help.
