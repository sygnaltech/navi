Visit the World Renowned Hobbiton Movie Set Tour

Embark on an unforgettable journey to the Hobbiton Movie Set, where the magic of The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings trilogies comes to life. At Navi Tours, we specialize in creating memorable experiences for fans and travelers alike. Whether you're looking for an immersive Hobbiton experience or a comprehensive New Zealand tour, we’ve got you covered.

Iconic New Zealand Tours: Hobbiton Movie Set

Step into the world of hobbits and enjoy a Hobbiton day tour like no other. Wander through lush landscapes and marvel at iconic sights such as the hobbit holes and the Dragon Inn. Sip on a refreshing drink at the Green Dragon Inn and immerse yourself in the authentic details that bring the Shire to life.

For those seeking a more personalized adventure, we offer small group tours designed to create an intimate experience. Our knowledgeable guides provide fascinating travel info, sharing behind-the-scenes stories and insights into the making of the movies. Whether you’re traveling from Auckland or exploring the North Island, Hobbiton is a destination you won’t want to miss.

More Than Just Hobbiton

Enhance your journey by combining your Hobbiton tour with a visit to other incredible destinations. We also offer tours to see the wonders of the Waitomo Caves, where glowing bioluminescent ceilings create a magical atmosphere. Explore Matamata, the charming town that serves as the gateway to Middle-earth. Whether you’re planning a full day tour or a quick getaway, our tours from Auckland are the perfect way to see it all.

Why Choose Navi Tours' Auckland to Hobbiton Experience?

At Navi Tours, we’re passionate about creating unforgettable memories. Whether you’re a fan of the movies or simply love breathtaking landscapes, our tours are tailored to suit your needs.

Plan your visit today and discover why Hobbiton is one of the most beloved destinations in the world. With Navi Tours, you’ll experience the magic, the beauty, and the stories of Middle-earth like never before.

Special Hobbiton Tour Notes

  • Includes full round-trip transportation from Auckland.
  • You can select the pick up and drop off hotel option from the following 5 hotels: 
    Cordis / J W Mariott / M social / Skycity / Pullman
  • This tour includes admission to the Hobbiton Movie Set and 1 drink at Green Dragon Inn.
  • We offer both English and Japanese-speaking drivers for our shuttle transport.
  • Hobbiton provides their own English-speaking local guides for that portion of the tour.

Special Note for this Tour

All terms & conditions below apply as normal, with these differences;

Booking

  • A minimum of 2 adults must be attending the tour for the tour to take place.
  • Application for a tour must be made by phone, fax or e-mail no later than 5:00 PM of the day before the tour.
  • Your application may not be accepted if the tour is full, prior to your booking.
  • Hobbiton Movie Set is closed on Christmas Day.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations and refunds are based on the working day we receive your request. Please note that requests must must be received before 5 PM ( NZT ) to be processed on that working day.

  • Cancellations made more than 72 hours before the tour start time are free of charge.
  • Cancellations made between 72 and 48 hours before the tour start time will incur a 50% cancellation fee.
  • Cancellations made within 48 hours of the tour start time will be charged the full tour fee.
  • If you are not present at the specified pick-up location at the departure time, the tour must depart without you.
  • No refunds will be made after travel has commenced.
  • Refunds for Paypal payments do not include card fees ( 3% ) as per Paypal terms & conditions.
The above cancellation policy may have exceptions for certain tours, which will be communicated to you at the time of booking.

Tour Costs

  • If there are two adults attending the tour, the cost of the tour written is the cost for one person.
  • If there are two adults attending the tour, children ( aged 5~11 ) will cost half of what is written.

Warning

  • Please understand that changes of schedule, cost and tours may occur with no notice.
  • Photos are only images.
