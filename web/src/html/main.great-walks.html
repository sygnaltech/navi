New Zealand at its Very Best

New Zealand's Great Walks are premier tracks that pass through diverse and spectacular scenery.

From native forests, lakes and rivers to rugged mountain peaks, deep gorges and vast valleys... there's a Great Walk for everyone!

Great Walks tracks are well formed and easy to follow. While most people prefer to explore on their own terms, guided trips offer a bit more comfort.

Great Walks are accessible from major towns that are well serviced by local operators and accommodation and transport providers.
