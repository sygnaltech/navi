Design Your Own Tour!

Custom New Zealand tours, anywhere you want to go

Want to go somewhere special?

Our expert tour guides will help you design your own unique tour.

Design your own unique tour experience, with our help & recommendations.

You can choose your own-

  • Dates of travel, including half-day, whole day, or multi-day tours.
  • Locations you want to visit, anywhere in New Zealand's North or South Islands.
  • Number of people you want to accommodate. Our custom tours are best suited for groups from 2 to 30+. We can accommodate all ages and situations. Ask us if you have special needs regarding transportation or diet.

How's that for service?

Get started now!

Here's how it works...

  1. Click the button below to launch our Custom Tour enquiry form.
  2. Enter your tour request details, some details about your tour group, when and where you want to visit and the types of experiences you're looking for.
  3. Our team will review it and put together some suggested options, a proposed itinerary, and pricing options for you. We'll make our recommendations based on your interests, participants & budget, as well as the season and weather.
  4. We'll deliver it by email within a few business days.
