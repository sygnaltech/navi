Meet the Ancient Guardians of Waipoua Forest — Tāne Mahuta & Te Matua Ngahere

Deep in Northland lies the sacred Waipoua Forest, home to mighty kauri trees that have stood for millennia. Here, the legendary “Lord of the Forest” Tāne Mahuta and the revered “Father of the Forest” Te Matua Ngahere watch over their timeless domain, inviting visitors into a place where nature, mythology, and history intertwine.

Your journey begins at the Kauri Museum, where you’ll uncover the stories of Kauri trees and their role in New Zealand’s early settlement. After a relaxing break at a local café, we head into the heart of the ancient forest — a world of deep green light, birdsong, and towering wooden giants.

Along the forest walk, you’ll encounter Te Matua Ngahere — estimated to be around 3,000 years old, with a massive 17-metre girth. Standing before this ancient guardian is a humbling experience, filled with awe and silence.
Further ahead awaits Tāne Mahuta, rising 55 metres high with a 14-metre trunk, commanding the forest like the god he is named after. He is spiritually linked to Japan’s Jōmon Sugi of Yakushima, symbolising a living bond between two ancient cultures.

In this forest shaped by the memory of the supercontinent Gondwana, experience a day that feels like stepping back millions of years — a rare chance to connect with primordial nature and the legends that live within it.
