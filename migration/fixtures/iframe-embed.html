Doubtful Sound is unspoiled, unmatched and unforgettable. So get ready to create some lifelong memories as the Fiordland Navigator takes you through the ultimate Doubtful Sound experience.

Spend a magical night aboard the Fiordland Navigator that sleeps 72 and enjoy the spacious viewing decks, dining salon, licensed bar and observation lounge. As you cruise through some of New Zealand’s most stunning scenery, our knowledgeable nature guides will provide commentary ensuring you miss nothing, be it the pod of resident dolphins, fur seals or rare penguins you might encounter.

Trip Plan

Tour Length: 24-27 hours

Minimum Number: From 1 person. If you would like to book a group charter, please contact us for a quote.

Cruise departure times: September – May

  • Manapouri (cruise only): 12pm
  • Te Anau (coach & cruise): 11am
  • Queenstown (coach & cruise): 9.00am

Cruise arrival times: September – May

  • Manapouri (cruise only): 11.45am
  • Te Anau (coach & cruise): 12.45pm
  • Queenstown (coach & cruise): 3.30pm

What to Bring: For the most enjoyable experience, we recommend that you bring:

  • Non-slip walking shoes/boots
  • Waterproof jacket*
  • Warm sweater/fleece jacket
  • Change of clothes*
  • Sunscreen/sunglasses
  • Insect repellent*
  • Swimwear (optional)
  • Camera
  • Cash for bar and snacks
  • Lunch and snacks or pre-order a lunch box for first day
  • Small bag only

* Essential items if you are doing activities. Activities take place even if it is raining.
