🌿 Matakana Tour Highlights

  1. Matakana Village
    Explore Matakana’s famous Saturday Market — filled with fresh produce, handcrafted treats, local wine, and charming boutique stalls that capture the spirit of this countryside town.‍
  2. Parry Kauri Park
    Wander through native bush and stand before majestic kauri trees — a peaceful nature experience showcasing New Zealand’s ancient giants.‍
  3. Omaha Beach Walk
    Stroll along pristine white sand beaches with crystal-clear waters, perfect for refreshing sea air and scenic photos.‍
  4. Local Gelato Shop Stop
    Treat yourself to rich, premium gelato made with quality local ingredients — a favourite spot among locals and visitors alike.‍
